Road trip planner · October 2026 · two travellers, one car

Biscay to Mediterranean

Bilbao to Barcelona over roughly nineteen nights, with twelve hours of unavoidable driving. This tool exists to answer one question: how many nights where, and what does each one cost somewhere else.

How this works a minute, then you can forget it

Really only two decisions: how many nights in each town, and what to do on each day. Everything else on the page is worked out from those two. Nothing you tap is permanent, so poke at it.

  1. Dates and flightsWhen we land, when we have to be in Barcelona, when the ship goes. Landing a day later means a night less to spend.
  2. Six ways to spend itReady-made starting points — mountains, coast, wine. Tap one and everything fills in. They are starting points, not commitments.
  3. Night allocatorHow many nights each town gets. The number of nights is fixed, so adding one takes one from somewhere else. The ◇ pin protects a town from being raided.
  4. What to actually doA hundred-odd places. Filter by the tags — nature, food, wine, ocean. Add one, then choose which day it happens on.
  5. The itineraryOne row per day, split into morning, afternoon and evening. This is the thing we are building.

This page is not running. Everything below — the elevation chart, the night allocator, the presets and all 112 places — is drawn by JavaScript, so in a preview pane that blocks scripts you get an empty shell with three date boxes that do nothing. Save roadtrip.html to disk and open it in a browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) and it will work. Nothing is fetched from the network except the fonts, so it runs fine offline.

What to actually do

Every entry carries a verified October 2026 operating status. Add anything to the plan; it appears in the day-by-day and the export.

The itinerary

One row per day. Empty slots are not a gap to fill — with this much slack they are the point.

On the research. October operating status was verified against official sources where reachable. Entries marked UNVERIFIED are ones where the official site could not be read directly — treat those as "phone before you drive". Distances and drive times are typical-conditions estimates for planning, not routing output. Toll figures are 2026 published car rates where confirmed; treat the running total as an order of magnitude.

Accommodation is deliberately out of scope. No hotels here, by design.
